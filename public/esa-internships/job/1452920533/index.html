What you'll do

You can choose among the following topics:

1) Topic 1: Process Automation and Digital Tools

  • identify opportunities to automate repetitive or manual processes within market research, financial assessments, and application tracking;
  • contribute to the design and development of simple internal tools (e.g., dashboards, templates, or scripts) to streamline data analysis and reporting;
  • support integration and upkeep of document repositories, or shared databases used in engagement and tracking efforts;
  • work with the team to pilot and evaluate automation tools to enhance operational efficiency.

2) Topic 2: Communication and Relationship with Industry

  • provide hands-on support to companies during their application process, offering clear guidance and responsive assistance;
  • collaborate with internal teams to create effective marketing materials, case studies, and outreach content tailored to different industry segments;
  • assist in organising and supporting industry events, webinars, and matchmaking activities that foster ecosystem engagement.

3) Topic 3: Space Market Research and Business Analysis

  • conduct research to identify and evaluate potential candidate companies within the space sector, supporting the development of a comprehensive and up-to-date ecosystem map;
  • analyse business plans submitted by applicant companies, assessing their viability, innovation potential, and alignment with evolving space industry trends;
  • support internal efforts to refine financial assessment processes, including data collection, scoring frameworks, and benchmarking practices;
  • monitor and report on emerging industry trends, contributing insights for future thematic programme campaigns and strategic focus areas.

What you need

You are proactive and autonomous with strong analytical and organisational skills. You are able to identify inefficiencies and propose automation solutions within existing workflows using Microsoft-based platforms.

You have knowledge of the financial ecosystem and an understanding of the investment lifecycle of a company, as well as knowledge of the European space ecosystem and space economy.

The following are considered assets:

  • experience with Microsoft 365 tools, particularly advanced Excel functions and macros, Power BI and Power Automate for process optimisation and reporting;
  • a demonstrated interest in space technologies and start-ups; practical experience gained in a space start-up;
  • knowledge and background in economics, finance or business administration;
  • knowledge and background in marketing and communication.

Plus the standard education and language requirements under “Applies to all ESA internships”.

About the team

The Access to Finance, Ventures and Analytics Office is responsible for developing measures to encourage, assist and ease access to finance for space entities and commercial projects, including support to venture capital investment and support to secure debt or loans. In addition, the office provides expertise in business analytics and automation, supporting both the operation and impact assessment of the CIP programmes and support schemes (including financing activities). It also contributes to the development of a comprehensive and effective commercial and institutional market intelligence, making full use of and coordinating with relevant areas across ESA.
