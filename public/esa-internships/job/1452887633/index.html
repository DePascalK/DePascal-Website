What you'll do

As an intern, you will be integrated in the Capability & Country Support Division and will participate in the following tasks:

  • process automation in SharePoint using Power Automate, Power Apps, Python and HTML/CSS/JavaScript;
  • web-based apps development using React;
  • Linux for the virtual machines;
  • financial data handling and Power BI dashboards development;
  • creation of supporting tools and procedures for the team to function more efficiently.

About the team

The BUC&PMSO Office Corporate Directorates supports the Capability & Country Support Division, Industrial Policy and Auditing Department, based at ESTEC, which is responsible for implementing integration measures for the New Member States (NMS), Associate Members (AM), and Cooperating States (PECS) in cooperation with Programme Directorates and the ESA Technical Directorate.

The Division manages in particular the Incentive Schemes and RPA Schemes which aim at developing and reinforcing sustainable industrial and academic capabilities in the space domain in these countries, through technology development contracts, selected through specific Calls for Activities, on topics related to ESA’s overall future programmes' activities.

Candidates interested are encouraged to visit the ESA website: http://www.esa.int
