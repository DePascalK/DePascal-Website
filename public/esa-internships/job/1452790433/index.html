What you'll do

You can choose between the following topics:

1) Topic 1: Maintenance and Enhancement of Pre-Engineering and Programme Management Tools

You will contribute to the maintenance, enhancement and deployment of internal STS-IF pre-engineering and programme management tools by:

  • maintaining and upgrading STS-IF internal engineering and programme management tools;
  • ensuring that tools can be easily installed and operated on ESA workstations and, where appropriate, made accessible through web-based interfaces;
  • improving tool robustness and compatibility with evolving Windows, Python and Streamlit environments;
  • developing new functionalities and enhancing existing features/functions based on user needs;
  • facilitating access to the tools for other ESA users;
  • preparing and delivering demonstrations and presentations of new functionalities;
  • assessing the feasibility of long-term maintenance of these tools by ESA IT services.

2) Topic 2: Identification of Artificial Intelligence Applications to streamline STS-IF activities

You will analyse STS-IF activities and develop proposals for the use of AI to improve efficiency and reduce administrative workload in areas such as:

  • action tracking and follow-up;
  • preparation of presentations and briefing materials;
  • equipment price monitoring and market intelligence;
  • automated generation of status reports.

3) Topic 3: Development of a Maintenance Planning and Prioritisation Tool for Test Facilities

You will design and develop a tool to support the management of propulsion test facility maintenance activities, including:

  • recording main maintenance tasks and related cost estimates;
  • prioritising activities based on operational needs and available resources;
  • generating summaries and management reporting to support decision-making.

What you need

Following skills are considered an advantage:

  • Python programming;
  • knowledge of Streamlit;
  • knowledge of AI;
  • knowledge of project management;
  • knowledge of fluid dynamics.

About the team

The Propulsion Test Facilities and Services team (STS-IF), part of the Infrastructure Department, is responsible for the stewardship of ESA-owned propulsion test facilities.

The team ensures that these facilities, operated by industry and public-sector partners, remain fully operational and available to support current and future propulsion development activities. It is also responsible for setting up, managing and monitoring the contractual framework with the different facility operators.

Candidates interested are encouraged to visit the ESA website: http://www.esa.int
