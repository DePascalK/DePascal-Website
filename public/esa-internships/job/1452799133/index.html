What you'll do

You will be based at the European Space Agency (ESA) Headquarters in Paris. During this internship, you will support the Head of the Space Safety Programme Coordination Office in a number of their duties through the preparation of reports, briefs, background material and presentations for the coordinators’ interactions with the rest of the Agency, ESA Member States and other partners (e.g. foreign agencies, industry, EU).

You will also be involved in the operations of the Strategic Space Safety Initiatives, especially on activities related to the Zero Debris Charter and space weather services.

What you need

Academic and/or practical experience in both engineering/science and social science would be a strong asset.

Due to the nature of the post, a commitment of six months is preferred, depending on the candidate’s availability.

About the team

The ESA Space Safety Department takes care of all potential hazards and threats from space or in space, which can impact human activities, infrastructure, or related applications. The spectrum of tasks ranges from gathering the basic understanding of space hazards originating from our Sun, from human space debris, or from asteroids potentially colliding with the Earth. It also addresses basic approaches to mitigate these hazards and to implement space systems end-to-end in a sustainable and eco-compatible way. Moreover, several corner stone missions are underway to demonstrate basic methods of space weather forecasting, asteroid deflection and active space debris removal from orbit.

In execution of these tasks, the Space Safety Department has the following entities:

  • Mission Implementation Division (OPS-SM);
  • COSMIC Project Management Office (OPS-SC);
  • Space Weather Office (OPS-SW);
  • Planetary Defence Office (OPS-SP);
  • Space Debris Office (OPS-SD);
  • Clean Space & Circular Economy Office (OPS-SY);
  • Frequency Management Office (OPS-SF);
  • Programme Coordination Office (OPS-SX).

The Space Safety Programme Coordination Office is responsible for leading strategic and coordination activities relating to the ESA Space Safety Programme, in close cooperation with the Programme division and offices, including but not restricted to programme strategy, identification of future activities, managing the programme board, overseeing international and European relations, managing industry relations, managing the programme’s communication activities, etc.
