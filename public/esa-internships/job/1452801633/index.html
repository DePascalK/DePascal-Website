What you'll do

You will be part of the FOX team, gaining insight into operational challenges and contributing to the development of novel solutions for future mission support. You will be actively involved in ongoing experimental activities, including system prototyping, testing, integration, and performance evaluation, and will participate in regular technical discussions and collaborative reviews across disciplines. The internship provides an opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a highly dynamic and interdisciplinary environment at the intersection of operations, engineering, and research.

You can choose between the following topics:

Topic 1: Integration and validation of Digital Intermediate Frequency Interoperability (DIFI) solutions

Activities include the use of Software Defined Radio (SDR) & Digitizers/Samplers to be integrated, configured and validated within the FOX operational environment. In particular, you will focus on the implementation of DIFI to enable SDR interoperability using a generic DIFI enabled sampler. You will learn how to configure and operate SDRs, especially for what concerns user-friendliness, sustainability and reliability aspects.

Topic 2: Harmonisation of ground station interfaces and protocols for enhanced interoperability

You contribute to the integration of external systems using different protocols into the centralized FOX ground segment environment. You will tailor and validate interfaces with the aim of improving interoperability aspects for both real-time telecommanding and telemetry reception, as well as tracking and scientific data downlink. The final goal is to centralise services and allow users to transparently adapt well-established standardardised protocols without the need to embark in developing custom interfaces. You will learn how mission users connect to ground stations with a special focus on interoperability aspects and their trade-offs for real operational environments.

What you need

  • Knowledge of Python or another relevant programming language;
  • general understanding of Radio Frequency communication systems and signal processing;
  • basic understanding of ground station operations;
  • familiarity with software defined radios and/or ground station protocols is an asset.

About the team

You will be directly contributing to the objectives of the Facility for Operations Experimentation (FOX) (https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Operations/Facility_for_Operations_Experimentation_FOX).

FOX, based at ESA’s European Space Operations Centre (ESOC) in Darmstadt, Germany, is an initiative designed to support operational experimentation, technology validation, in-orbit demonstration/validation (IOD/IOV), qualification activities, end-to-end operational assessment and experimentation support for both traditional and New Space missions.

FOX facilitates experimentation, qualification, interoperability and operational validation activities that may be difficult to perform within purely operational service environments. Experimental applications, services and concepts can be tested and progressively adopted by mainstream missions. Furthermore, FOX contributes to increased interoperability and operational readiness across the evolving New Space ecosystem.

FOX supports ESA internal users as well as universities, research institutes and companies prototyping, testing and validating innovative operations concepts and technologies by fostering innovation, experimentation, and cross-functional collaboration in the domain of ground operations. Typical users benefitting from FOX services span from traditional space missions to CubeSats and SmallSats manufacturers and operators, as well as Ground segment developers and ground station service providers.
