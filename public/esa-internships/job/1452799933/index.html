What you'll do

You will design and implement Python scripts, tools and frameworks that support routine spacecraft operations and automate recurring tasks. Your tools will be used by the Flight Control Team in a live mission environment and will contribute directly to safer and more efficient operations.

The exact scope of the activities will be decided together with you, depending on your profile, interests, and the mission needs. Possible tasks include both development of new tools, as well as adaptation of existing tools to make them generic and improve harmonisation across the different missions. Example use cases include automating daily checks of spacecraft state, the generation of reports, and creating dashboards, as well as tools for modelling expected spacecraft states.

These activities will include extracting relevant data from ESOC internal interfaces, processing and validating it, and presenting the results clearly to operators. The resulting tools should be packaged in a reusable Python library with helpful documentation and examples. You will also share your results with the team through short demonstrations or training sessions.

During your internship, you will learn about the mission control systems, tools, and interfaces used at ESOC. You will gain hands-on experience in how satellite operations are planned and conducted during routine activities, with direct exposure to the Flight Control Team and the control room environment.

What you need

What you bring:

  • Strong Python skills from a computer science or engineering background, with interest in automation and data processing;
  • motivation to write clear, tested and well‑documented code;
  • curiosity about space systems and the motivation to support real‑world operations through practical software;

About the team

In this internship, you will become part of the Flight Control Teams responsible for flight operations of the FLEX, Biomass, EarthCARE, Swarm, and CryoSat missions at the European Space Operations Centre in Darmstadt, Germany. These missions are part of the Earth Explorer family, focused on advancing Earth science through observation of Earth’s key systems.

The Flight Control Team is responsible for conducting day-to-day operations of these missions, working closely across ground segment and science interfaces to ensure that the missions deliver high-quality data to the scientific community. During the routine operations phase of the missions, the team develops and relies on a set of tools and processes that make operations safer, faster and more efficient, balancing reliability and innovation, while maintaining robust space and ground segment operations.

As the intern in our team, you will join an international, collaborative and supportive environment. You will gain direct exposure to real spacecraft operations and experience how software, automation, and operational processes are used in a unique control centre environment.
