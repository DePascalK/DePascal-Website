What you'll do

Through this internship, you will gain, particularly in the context of the automotive and micro-mobility sectors:

  1. a comprehensive overview of innovative services based on integrated space-based data and terrestrial technologies;
  2. an in depth understanding of future trends in the automotive industry and the emerging role of the space industry in the automotive value chain;
  3. experience in business development and an understanding of business models.

You will aim to build a comprehensive understanding of the market opportunities in the mobility sector for downstream space applications to support the development of sustainable, green and innovative applications. The emphasis will be on the automotive and micro-mobility industries, where ESA has a number of working groups and initiatives with external partners.

As an intern, you will have the following tasks:

  • identifying and characterising commercial opportunities for innovative services and applications in the mobility sector exploiting space assets such as satellite communications, satellite navigation, and earth observation;
  • supporting the identification of and engagement with champion industry stakeholders and public entities that may become potential customers and users of the mobility services identified;
  • supporting the characterisation, documentation and organisation of relevant industrial contacts and stakeholders;
  • identifying gaps in existing technologies and services and supporting the identification of new developments in both terrestrial and space-based technologies, including emerging technologies.

What you need

The following are considered assets:

  • knowledge of the automotive sector;
  • knowledge of business development and commercial topics;
  • an interest in new and emerging technologies.

About the team

ESA Business Applications and Space Solutions (BASS) aims to demonstrate that space is open for business and has the power to improve our everyday lives on Earth. This is done by helping companies in ESA’s member states develop innovative services and products that utilise data from existing space assets, such as satellite communication, earth observation, satellite navigation and human spaceflight technologies, in combination with terrestrial technologies, to deliver sustainable services for the benefit of user communities.

Operating at the intersection of innovation and business, the programme helps stakeholders address commercial and technical risks throughout the development lifecycle, transforming commercially promising space‐based application concepts into operational, profitable and self‐sustaining applications. Acting as a trusted mediator between user communities, the space industry, and service providers, ESA BASS is committed to demonstrating the transformative power of space technology in improving everyday life on Earth.

Mobility is one of the sectors with significant potential for innovative services that integrate space technologies. The green transition currently under way, together with the digitalisation and automation of transport, brings countless opportunities for innovation across the entire chain.
