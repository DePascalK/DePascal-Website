What you'll do

As a Contracts intern, you would have the opportunity to gain first-hand exposure to the legal and contractual challenges arising from innovative space projects, while developing an understanding of how legal expertise can contribute to the development, deployment and commercialisation of space technologies and applications.

You would learn alongside contract officers and specialists from different nationalities and professional backgrounds, in a collaborative and international environment. This is an opportunity to combine your legal skills with an interest in cutting-edge space technology and to see how contracts and legal frameworks can help transform innovative ideas and space assets into real-world satellite applications.

You will learn how procurements are performed at an international level, you will understand what the requirements to launch a public procurement are, what is the best procurement procedure for your objective, how to make sure that the procurement is performed according to the principle of fairness, ensuring equal treatment of the involved bidders and how to select the best offer with respect to the budget available.

The experience you will gain may help you further to go on working in the legal practice as a lawyer or as legal counsel in a company dedicated to the booming space sector. During your internship, you will prepare contracts, adapting them to the objectives of the procurements.

About the team

The team you would be joining is a dynamic and international group of legal professionals who share a strong passion for space and for the transformative potential of space technology. Our work sits at the intersection of law, technology and innovation, supporting projects that turn space capabilities into practical applications and services on Earth.

In particular, the team is involved in the contractual and legal aspects of space technology application projects, including projects making use of satellite-based assets and services. We work closely with technical teams and other experts to understand the objectives of each project and identify the most appropriate contractual and legal solutions to support their successful implementation.
