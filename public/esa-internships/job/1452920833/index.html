What you'll do

Under the direct guidance of the Head of the Space Resilience Office, you will support activities at the interface between engineering, security analysis and policy, helping the team assess threats to space systems, understand their strategic implications and position resilience activities within Europe's wider security and defence framework.

This opportunity is therefore suited to candidates from either a technical or policy background who are motivated to develop a multidisciplinary perspective.

You will contribute to security and threat analysis within the Space Resilience Office of the Directorate of Resilience, Navigation and Connectivity, supporting activities that may combine technical assessment with policy and geopolitical analysis. As an intern, you will have the following tasks:

  • monitoring and analysing technical, security, policy and geopolitical developments relevant to European satellite navigation and secure connectivity programmes;
  • supporting the analysis of threats affecting space systems, including cyber threats, radio-frequency interference, jamming, spoofing and vulnerabilities across space, ground and user segments;
  • contributing to security, risk and resilience assessments of space systems, services and architectures, including the identification of vulnerabilities and potential mitigation measures;
  • tracking policy, regulatory and strategic developments affecting European space security and resilience activities;
  • supporting the assessment of security and geopolitical risks associated with international partnerships, critical technologies and supply chains;
  • preparing concise briefings, structured assessments and strategic positioning material for management, governing bodies and stakeholder meetings;
  • maintaining a structured repository of open-source information on threats, incidents, technologies and geopolitical developments relevant to the Office's activities;
  • supporting coordination with other Agency teams and with European institutions, Member States, industry, academia and international partners on security-related matters.

Through this internship, you will learn how technical evidence, threat analysis and geopolitical context are combined to support decisions on European space security and resilience.

You will also develop skills in multidisciplinary analysis, concise strategic communication and cooperation with technical and institutional stakeholders.

What you need

The following are considered assets:

  • knowledge and background in space telecommunications and navigation domains;
  • familiarity with threat and risk analysis;
  • knowledge of international relations and space treaties.

About the team

The Directorate of Resilience, Navigation and Connectivity supports Europe's ability to anticipate, withstand and respond to threats affecting satellite navigation and secure connectivity services that underpin everyday life, including transport, energy, health, defence and civil protection. This requires close engagement with European institutions, Member States, industry, academia and international partners to build a coordinated and resilient European space posture.
As Europe's reliance on space systems grows, so does its exposure to cyber attacks, jamming and spoofing of satellite signals, physical interference with ground and space infrastructure, supply-chain dependencies and wider geopolitical tensions. Understanding both the technical characteristics of these threats and their policy and geopolitical context is essential to keep European navigation and connectivity services safe, dependable and resilient.
