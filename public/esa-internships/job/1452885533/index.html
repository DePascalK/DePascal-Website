What you'll do

You will support contracting activities for the Science Programme.

You will help review procurement documentation and contractual provisions;

analyse contractual, programmatic and procedural questions;

compare approaches across different contract types;

and contribute to active procurement cases under the supervision of a contracts officer, with due regard to operational and time-efficiency as well as continuous evolutions.

By the end of the internship, you will understand the main stages of public procurement in the space sector, apply contractual and regulatory principles and collaborate effectively with technical, legal and programmatic stakeholders.

Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written.

About the team

The Science Contracts Service supports the European Space Agency’s Science Programme by delivering effective, transparent and timely procurement for scientific missions, studies, technology activities and mission operations.

Our focus is to help Science teams move efficiently from contract planning to contract signature and implementation, while maintaining sound governance, fair competition and value for money, including (but not limited to) for the upcoming exciting missions of LISA, EnVision, NewAthena.

Within the Contracts Department, the CFO-CX Contracts Division is in charge of the implementation of all activities in support of the Science and the Human and Robotic Exploration Programmes Directorates. The relevant contracts cover a wide range of complexity, from a few 100 K€ R&D to large Satellites Projects of several 100 M€.

The Division implements all related contracting tasks, ranging from tendering, planning and issue of invitations to tender, to evaluation of proposals, negotiation of contracts as well as their signature and monitoring.

Also, in parallel the Division contributes to the improvement of the overall Regulatory frame in the Contracts Department, and supports the general work initiated in the Department to improve time to contract.

You will join an international team at the European Space Research and Technology Centre in Noordwijk in the Netherlands, collaborating with contracts officers and engaging with Science stakeholders.

The environment combines procurement, legal and programmatic analysis, collaboration and continuous improvement.
