What you'll do

How do we engage diverse audiences across Europe? How can we make the best use of digital platforms to share compelling stories about space? What types of outreach activities help bring these stories to life and connect with people?

By joining the Communications team at ESA's Establishment in Italy (ESRIN), you will gain hands-on experience in digital communications and public outreach. Working alongside experienced professionals, you will develop your understanding of how international organisations create and deliver effective, integrated communication campaigns in a multilingual environment.

Your main tasks will include:

  • supporting, in close collaboration with the Public Relations team, the organisation of the School Days taking place in March and April and the organisation of the ESA Open Day, taking place at the end of September.

Additional tasks will include:

  • assisting with organisational and logistical activities;
  • drafting communications articles in English and Italian about PR events taking place at ESRIN;
  • identifying and contacting event stakeholders;
  • liaising with colleagues and other stakeholders within the Communication Department.

What you need

Due to the nature of this position, native-level proficiency in italian is mandatory.

Plus the standard education and language requirements under “Applies to all ESA internships”.

About the team

The ESA Member States Outreach Office interfaces and coordinates the ESA Member States Advisory Communication Committee to the Director General (ACC), while ensuring the harmonisation, development and implementation of public and media relations activities as well as social media coverage across the various ESA establishments and Member States.
The team, based at ESA ESRIN in Frascati, Italy, focuses on communication activities for the ESRIN establishment as well as for Italy, Greece, Hungary, Romania and Slovenia.
They work with governments, the press and the general public to inform and educate about all of our space activities, from astronauts to asteroids, Earth observation to satellites flying to Jupiter.

Candidates interested are encouraged to visit the ESA website: http://www.esa.int
