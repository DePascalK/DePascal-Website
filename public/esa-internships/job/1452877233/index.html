What you'll do

1) Your first core task will involve a research-based exercise:

About 21% of the world’s forest area is currently designated under legal protection. These forests are highly valuable for carbon storage and sustaining biodiversity. However, high annual rates of tree cover loss continue to this day, with impacts both on forest carbon stocks and their biodiversity. It therefore remains difficult to assess how effective protected area designation is as a forest conservation tool. The legal status of a protected area does not automatically mean that active protection is being implemented, particularly in regions with limited financial resources for conservation.

ESA’s Sentinel missions are widely used to monitor forests by providing frequent, consistent, and freely available satellite data on forests. In April 2025, ESA also launched the BIOMASS mission with a novel P-band synthetic aperture radar designed to deliver crucial information about forest conditions, changes, and their role in the carbon cycle.

You will utilise BIOMASS mission data in synergy with several other EO missions (particularly the Sentinels) to achieve the following:

  • develop forest (a) carbon and (b) structural/functional biodiversity indicators for protected areas;
  • map land cover and land cover change within protected boundaries using long-term EO time series, comparing trends inside protected areas with surrounding unprotected lands;
  • evaluate ecosystem condition, for example by tracking vegetation health, fragmentation, or recovery after disturbance;
  • summarise key findings that will concisely and accurately inform international/national policies aimed at forest conservation.

2) Your second task will involve support activities to the Applications Section of EOP, Green Solutions Division. These tasks are aimed at developing management skills and soft skills, supporting the overall career development of the candidate. Some activities include:

  • design and preparation of materials for conferences/meetings held at ESRIN;
  • participation in project meetings, assisting in review of ESA project reports;
  • participation in inter-section meetings (e.g. with ESA PhiLab), co-development of research ideas and agendas.

You will utilise ESA-supported cloud computing platforms for conducting your research. By the end of the internship, you will demonstrate practical experience in scalable Earth Observation processing, applied analytical methods for land monitoring, product validation and uncertainty assessment, and stakeholder-driven development in an international setting. You will also develop a broader understanding of how EO-derived forest information supports international policy and reporting frameworks.

What you need

In addition to the standard requirements in the form, the following would be considered an asset:

  • experience with Earth Observation for land cover mapping and multi temporal satellite data processing; familiarity with forest ecosystems is an asset;
  • strong programming skills (e.g. R, Python), including geospatial data processing;
  • knowledge of machine learning and deep learning for image and time series analysis;
  • experience with cloud or high performance computing environments;
  • ability to communicate results clearly in writing and presentations and to collaborate effectively with international partners.

About the team

The Applications Section is primarily engaged in research and development (R&D) for Earth observation-based solutions that address both European and global environmental and development agendas.

The typical projects of the EOP-SGA Section involve key stakeholders throughout the co-design, development, and validation phases to ensure that the innovative EO-based solutions are effectively integrated into end-users’ operational systems and practices.

The Applications Section, based at the ESRIN site of ESA, comprises several EO experts, each overseeing specific domains where EO data is utilised, including marine, carbon, and agriculture, among others.

Some of the activities are presented here: eo4society - eo science for society.
