What you'll do

As an intern in the External Relations Department, you will have the unique opportunity to contribute directly to the management of the Agency’s relations with its international partners.

This includes research on geopolitical and geostrategic developments of relevance to ESA and the space sector more broadly; foreign policy analysis; contribution to the formulation of internal positions and support to the implementation of ESA’s missions and programmes jointly carried out with international partners, in coordination with relevant programme directorates; and support to the handling of high-level meetings with international delegations.

In doing so, you will be fully integrated into the team, contribute to the practice of international relations in a dynamic period of global change, and gain insights into the functioning of an intergovernmental organisation at the heart of the space sector.

What you need

Knowledge of the global space sector, relevant foreign policies or international relations is an asset.

About the team

The External Relations Department shapes and delivers ESA’s international engagement strategy with non-Member States, their institutions, and international organisations. It develops and coordinates cooperation frameworks, supports high-level exchanges and representation – including through ESA offices in Washington and Tokyo – and provides strategic, political and diplomatic advice on international relations to senior management.

By monitoring geopolitical developments, preparing key engagements and ensuring alignment with Agency priorities, the Department plays a central role in advancing ESA’s international partnerships and positioning the Agency as a trusted and influential global partner.
